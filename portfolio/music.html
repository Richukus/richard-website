Programme Note

Draighean is the Irish name for blackthorn. The blackthorn is associated with the dark side of the year. It is a prickly shrub with long thorns that can cause septic flesh wounds. It produces bitter sweet berries (sloes) that ripen after the first frost.The blackthorn is often associated with overcoming obstacles for a better future or protection and hope in the midst of devastation.
