Portfolio

My practice combines composition, performance, creative technology, artistic direction and visual design. Through collaboration I create works that explore the relationships between sound, movement, interactive systems and digital media.

Current Project

Sound & Movement

A research and development project bringing together dancers and computer musicians to create new wearable technologies for performance. The project explores gesture capture, sonification and collaborative composition, resulting in a series of new interdisciplinary performance works.
