Choreographies of Sound

Choreographies of Sound, rehearsal, 2026.

Overview

Choreographies of Sound explores how physical movement can become a source for musical and visual transformation.

The project brings together dancers, musicians and interactive technologies to create a performance environment in which movement influences sound in real time.

Rather than creating a direct one-to-one translation between gesture and sound, the project explores more ambiguous relationships between physical action, data and musical behaviour.

Development

Developing the work

The project developed through a series of research and development sessions involving movement artists and computer musicians.

Early experiments focused on capturing movement using wearable inertial sensors. Data from the sensors was transmitted wirelessly and used as material for sound generation and processing.

This process became an opportunity to question how movement data might be interpreted musically, rather than simply mapped directly onto conventional musical parameters.

Technology

The technological system uses wearable inertial measurement units to capture aspects of the performer's movement.

Sensor data is transmitted wirelessly to Max/MSP, where it can be analysed and transformed into musical parameters.

The system is deliberately designed to allow for indirect and unstable relationships between movement and sound. Small physical changes can accumulate over time and influence larger musical structures.
